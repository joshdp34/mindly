Mindly Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Mindly is a mobile puzzle app that provides daily puzzle games, archives, streaks, stats, and spoiler-free sharing. This Privacy Policy explains how Mindly handles information when you use the app.

Who We Are

Mindly is operated by Joshua Patrick.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Mindly’s privacy practices, contact us at mindlypuzzles@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Mindly is designed to work without requiring an account.

Information Stored on Your Device

Mindly stores puzzle progress and settings locally on your device, including:

  • Completed puzzles
  • In-progress puzzle state
  • Guess history
  • Streaks and stats
  • Archive completion status
  • Theme preference
  • Sound and touch vibration preferences
  • Daily reminder preference and reminder time

This information is used to make the app work, preserve your progress, calculate stats, and show your results.

Puzzle Content Loaded From Our Server

Mindly may connect to our puzzle content service to download daily puzzle data, archive puzzle data, and Graphle puzzle images. This content is currently delivered through Supabase.

When your device connects to this service, standard technical information may be processed by Supabase or hosting providers, such as:

  • IP address
  • Device and browser/app request metadata
  • Date and time of requests
  • Requested content URLs

We use this connection only to deliver app content and keep the app functioning.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Mindly may use your device’s notification system to remind you to play. Notification preferences are stored on your device. You can turn reminders off in the app settings or in your device settings.

Subscriptions

Mindly may offer a monthly paid subscription for archive access. Subscriptions are processed by the app store platform, such as Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card information.

The app may store or check subscription status, entitlement status, product identifiers, purchase tokens, renewal status, or similar purchase-related information needed to provide archive access and manage subscription features. This information is used only to provide paid features, restore purchases, prevent unauthorized access, and support subscription-related customer service.

Information We Do Not Collect

Mindly does not currently:

  • Require user accounts
  • Collect your name, email address, or phone number through the app
  • Collect precise location
  • Access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • Use third-party advertising
  • Use third-party analytics
  • Sell personal information

How We Use Information

Mindly uses information to:

  • Provide daily puzzles and archive puzzles
  • Save your puzzle progress
  • Calculate streaks and stats
  • Display completed results
  • Support spoiler-free sharing
  • Remember app settings
  • Deliver optional daily reminders
  • Provide and verify monthly subscription access to archived puzzles if that feature is enabled
  • Maintain app reliability and security

Sharing Results

Mindly includes spoiler-free sharing. When you use the share button, the app creates share text on your device. You choose where to send it. Shared results are not automatically sent to us.

Third-Party Services

Mindly may use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: used to host and deliver puzzle content and images.
  • Google Play: used for app distribution and, if enabled, payment processing for archive access.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

Puzzle progress, stats, and settings remain on your device until you delete the app, clear app storage, or reset the app data if such a feature is available.

Server request logs may be retained by Supabase or hosting providers according to their policies and operational needs.

Children’s Privacy

Mindly is family-friendly, but it is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at mindlypuzzles@gmail.com.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the app and the services that provide puzzle content. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can:

  • Disable daily reminders in the app settings or device settings
  • Change theme, sound, and touch vibration settings in the app
  • Delete locally stored app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in your device settings
  • Choose whether to share puzzle results

International Users

Mindly may be used in different regions. Puzzle content services and app store services may process information in countries other than your own.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
